## Circuits

The RC time constant sets how fast a capacitor charges: after one tau it is at 63 percent, after five it is essentially full. The low-pass cutoff is 1/(2 pi R C), so a slower circuit also filters more.

## Signals

A square wave contains only odd harmonics, with amplitudes falling as 1/n. Truncating the series rings near the edges (the Gibbs overshoot, about 9 percent of the jump), and adding terms does not remove it.
