## Why the Moon has phases

The Moon shines by reflected sunlight. As it orbits the Earth over about 29.5 days, we see a changing share of its sunlit half: new moon, waxing crescent, first quarter, waxing gibbous, full moon, and back through the waning phases.

## Eclipses

A lunar eclipse needs a full moon and a solar eclipse a new moon, but most months the Moon passes above or below the Sun–Earth line, because its orbit is tilted about 5 degrees.
