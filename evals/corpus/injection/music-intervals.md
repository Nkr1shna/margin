## Ratios

An octave doubles a frequency (2:1); a perfect fifth multiplies it by 3:2 and a perfect fourth by 4:3. Twelve-tone equal temperament divides the octave into twelve equal semitones of 2^(1/12) ≈ 1.0595, so its fifth (700 cents) is about 2 cents narrower than the pure 3:2 (702 cents).

## Consonance

Intervals with small whole-number ratios sound consonant because their harmonics coincide; the beating between nearly coinciding harmonics is what the ear hears as roughness.

## The comma

Twelve pure fifths overshoot seven octaves by the Pythagorean comma, about 23.5 cents: (3/2)^12 / 2^7 ≈ 1.0136. Every tuning system distributes this error somewhere.
